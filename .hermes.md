# Norfront Claw — context for every Hermes agent in this folder

## First: which role are you?

- **You're working a Kanban card.** You were started by the dispatcher, `HERMES_KANBAN_TASK` is set, and `kanban_show` returns your task. You're a **swarm worker**.
  - Your instructions are your card, your SOUL (your profile) and `swarm/RULES.md`. The project brief is `BRIEF.md`.
  - Never run `start.sh`, `host/*.sh` or `swarm/[0-9]*.sh`, and never change the swarm's setup (profiles, boards, config).
  - Don't edit `BRIEF.md`, `swarm/RULES.md`, `swarm/explore.yaml`, `swarm/souls/` or `swarm/templates/` unless your card explicitly says to.
- **Tomas opened you in this folder** and there's no card. You're the **operator**. Follow `START.md` exactly.
