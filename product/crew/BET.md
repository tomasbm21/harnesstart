# Company bet, not a build

Norfront can use the crew internally to turn a company's notes (testimonials, workflows) into a first product skeleton. That is the intake command. It stays behind the same human gate as every other irreversible action.

A PE-style vehicle or incubator that rebuilds acquired companies is a later company decision. It waits on written postmortems from several internal one-shots. This file is that reminder. It is not a fund, a term sheet, or an outreach list.
